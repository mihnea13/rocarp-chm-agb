RoCarp-CHM-AGB

Canopy height and aboveground biomass of the Romanian Carpathian forests, wall to wall, at 10 m resolution.

RoCarp-CHM-AGB maps two forest structural variables — canopy height and aboveground biomass — across the forests of the Romanian Carpathian arc at 10 m resolution.

The maps are produced by machine-learning models calibrated against spaceborne LiDAR from NASA's GEDI mission and applied to a predictor stack that combines optical imagery, C-band and L-band radar, and terrain data. The CatBoost product carries a per-pixel predictive uncertainty layer estimated by the model itself, so that users can tell where the prediction is well constrained and where it is not.

The data are not yet public. They will be deposited on Zenodo under a permanent DOI, together with the peer-reviewed publication, in the first quarter of 2027. This page is the entry point — the download link and the DOI will appear here. Nothing else needs to be bookmarked.

On the uncertainty layer

The CatBoost product carries a second band with the model’s own predictive standard deviation, obtained from a gradient-boosted model fitted with a heteroscedastic loss. It estimates the dispersion the model assigns to each individual prediction, and is therefore spatially informative rather than constant.

It is not a complete prediction interval: it excludes the reference-data uncertainty of the GEDI footprint estimates, the geolocation error propagated into predictor extraction, and the structural error of the model family.

Method

  1. Reference data. GEDI L2A and L4A footprints, quality-filtered, with footprint geolocation refined against a high-resolution digital terrain model.
  2. Predictors. Seasonal Sentinel-2 composites and vegetation indices; Sentinel-1 C-band backscatter; ALOS-2 PALSAR-2 L-band annual mosaic; elevation, slope and aspect derived from the ANCPI digital terrain model of Romania.
  3. Feature selection. Recursive feature elimination with cross-validation over the full multi-source stack.
  4. Training. Spatially blocked cross-validation on a 30 × 30 km grid, hyperparameters tuned by Optuna.
  5. Prediction. Block-wise application to the full raster stack, restricted to the forest mask.

The complete methodological description will accompany the publication.

Data sources

  • GEDI L2A / L4A — NASA Global Ecosystem Dynamics Investigation, LP DAAC and ORNL DAAC
  • Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 — modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2024–2025), processed by the authors
  • ALOS-2 PALSAR-2 yearly mosaic — © JAXA/METI; cite Shimada et al. (2014)
  • Dynamic World — Google / World Resources Institute, CC BY 4.0
  • Digital terrain model — Romania, 1 / 5 / 10 m, distributed through the ANCPI geoportal; gap-filled and resampled to 10 m by the authors

The predictor stack itself is not redistributed. Only the derived canopy height and biomass products are released, together with the code needed to reproduce them from the original sources.
